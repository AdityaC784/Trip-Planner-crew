## **Packing Suggestions for October in Kyoto**

### **Clothing**
- **Layers**: Light sweater, cardigan, or fleece jacket
- **Comfortable walking shoes** (you'll walk 15,000+ steps daily)
- **Light rain jacket** or compact umbrella
- **Long pants** and comfortable jeans
- **Light long-sleeve shirts**
- **One dressy outfit** for fine dining experiences

### **Essential Items**
- **Portable phone charger** (long sightseeing days)
- **Camera** with extra memory cards
- **Cash** (many places don't accept cards)
- **JR Pass** (if planning day trips)
- **Temple etiquette guide** printed copy
- **Comfortable day backpack**

### **Cultural Items**
- **Respectful attire** for temples (covered shoulders/knees)
- **Slip-on shoes** (easier at temples requiring shoe removal)
- **Small gift from your home country** (for any local interactions)

---

## **Safety Tips & Cultural Notes**

### **Temple Etiquette**
- Bow before entering temple grounds
- Remove hats and sunglasses
- No flash photography inside buildings
- Speak quietly and respectfully

### **Gion District Rules**
- **No photography** on private streets with geishas
- Maintain respectful distance from geishas
- Don't block narrow pathways

### **General Safety**
- Kyoto is extremely safe - crime rates are very low
- Keep cash handy (many places don't accept cards)
- Learn basic Japanese phrases: "Arigato gozaimasu" (thank you)
- Emergency number: 110 (police), 119 (fire/ambulance)
